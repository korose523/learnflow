# Covariance Weighting and Residualization in Difficulty Fusion

Zexiao Weng

## Scope

This methods supplement examines covariance-based weighting, residualization and criterion dependence in cached difficulty-fusion experiments. It is not an independent journal submission. It separates an exact population statement from an implemented rank-based heuristic and presents every evaluated sample size. The analysis introduces no new participants, model inference or learning-effect claims.

## 1 Population covariance weights

Let z be a centered vector of indicators, t a centered latent variable with Var(t)=1, V=Cov(z) positive definite, and q=Cov(z,t). For c=βᵀz, the squared correlation is

corr(c,t)² = (βᵀq)² / (βᵀVβ)

Applying Cauchy–Schwarz to V¹ᐟ²β and V⁻¹ᐟ²q gives an upper bound qᵀV⁻¹q. Equality is attained when β is proportional to V⁻¹q. The scale is unidentified by this objective, and its sign is chosen to align c with t. This result concerns population covariance and the named latent target. It does not imply that any empirical proxy for V or q optimizes teacher-labelled difficulty.

Under independent single-factor errors, z_k=q_kt+ε_k, with q_k=s_k√ρ_k, Var(ε_k)=1−ρ_k, and uncorrelated errors, V=qqᵀ+D where D=diag(1−ρ_k). For 0≤ρ_k<1, the matrix inversion identity gives

V⁻¹q = D⁻¹q / (1 + qᵀD⁻¹q),     β_k ∝ s_k√ρ_k / (1−ρ_k)

By contrast, V=I gives β=q. Multiple nonzero independent-error loadings produce off-diagonal covariance q_iq_j, so setting V=I is generally incompatible with that single-factor model. Neither expression is proportional to ρ in general. For ρ=(0.25,0.81), normalized independent-error weights are (0.12338,0.87662), direct reliability weights are (0.23585,0.76415), and identity-covariance loading weights are (0.35714,0.64286). Therefore srw7 is not a mathematical special case of covariance weighting. Accidental equality in restricted parameter settings does not establish a general nesting relation.

## 2 Implementation and validity boundaries

The existing implementation substitutes a Spearman rank-correlation matrix for V, estimated split-half stability for the loading proxy, and a ridge term for numerical regularization. It combines transformed indicator values. These objects do not necessarily satisfy V=Cov(z) and q=Cov(z,t) for a common identified latent variable. The implemented estimator is thus a heuristic; the population proof is not a proof of its empirical optimality.

Reliability and criterion validity also require distinct targets. If v=t+η, with η independent of t and c, then

corr(c,v) = corr(c,t) / √[1 + Var(η)]

The correlations are not generally equal. If auxiliary variables measure another stable construct, even this attenuation relation need not apply. High split-half stability cannot establish correspondence to teacher difficulty. A teacher label is itself a particular external criterion, not an error-free universal difficulty value.

The absolute auxiliary coefficient share, Σ|β_aux|/Σ|β|, describes the fitted coefficients. It is not the minimizer of prediction error or an identified optimal mixture budget. Likewise, residualization removes a fitted linear component shared with the reference; it cannot demonstrate incremental validity without a criterion comparison. Spearman–Brown coverage extrapolation [2] requires assumptions about parallel observations and error structure and does not resolve informative missingness.

## 3 Exact pairwise aggregation statement

For finite item scores u, suppose the pairwise fractional evidence on a connected comparison graph is W_ij=σ(u_i−u_j). In the Bradley–Terry likelihood [1], the gradient at d=u vanishes. Its negative Hessian is a weighted graph Laplacian and is positive definite on the zero-sum subspace. The identified solution is therefore d=u−mean(u), rather than uncentered u.

If log-odds are averaged over m sources, the corresponding solution is centered u/m. Its ranking is unchanged for m>0. This exact identity concerns fractional evidence generated by the same score differences. Sampled Bernoulli outcomes do not generally give exact finite-sample recovery. Probability-domain averaging or pair-specific missingness may break additive separability, but they need not change ranks in every case and do not automatically improve criterion agreement. A three-line numerical identity test cannot demonstrate how an LLM internally aggregates evidence.

The full covariance heuristic also showed poor agreement with DBE teacher labels in the historical experiment, while showing better agreement with Junyi platform labels. Different datasets, signals and label processes prevent attribution of this contrast solely to changing the criterion.

Repeated response subsamples reuse items, students and labels. Their variation can describe conditional sensitivity to response selection, but repeated-subsample paired t statistics and standardized effects are not independent population evidence. They are omitted from this supplement. A high win fraction over those subsamples is similarly conditional on the same cache and fitting procedure.

## 5 New conditional paired-item evaluation

A locally specified exploratory evaluation draws one response sample per item at each k=10,25,50,100,200. It uses the existing prepare/all_variants implementation, the same draw for all estimators, seed 20261007 for DBE and 20261008 for Junyi, and no label-tuned lambda. The success, srw7_cf, m4_full, m4_E_cf and m4_E_rel score vectors are retained. Each difference from success receives 2,000 paired-item percentile bootstrap resamples, with average-rank/tie-aware Kendall τ_b supplied by SciPy.

The bootstrap treats sampled item units as exchangeable and conditions on cached responses and fitted scores. It does not refit transformations or weights, resample students, account for all topic clustering, or correct for multiple comparisons. It therefore excludes substantial sources of uncertainty. The new results are separate from historical repeated-subsample means and M1's full-transaction IRT analysis; values from those analyses must not be spliced together.

## 6 Implications for the main manuscript

The defensible connection to M1 is a methodological boundary: rank-scale commensurability does not identify criterion-optimal fusion weights. The covariance objective clarifies an assumption set; the negative and mixed results show why an external criterion remains necessary. Incorporating this supplement into M1 would require checking narrative scope and avoiding redundant experiments. The current document does not restore M4 as an independent submission or claim a new general estimator theorem.

## Reproducibility and study scope

The replication package stores the unchanged historical JSON, cache hash, computational source hashes, locally specified evaluation protocol, all fitted vectors and labels, conditional intervals, and numerical theory tests. The script is results/m4/recheck_m4.py. It uses existing public-data-derived cache files without new data collection. The cache is not a replacement for reconstructing the entire source-data pipeline.
